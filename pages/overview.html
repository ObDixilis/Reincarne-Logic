Core Principles

Our practice is rooted in ritual literacy, narrative coherence, and collective stewardship. These principles shape every decision, from initial discovery conversations through integration of new habits.

Reciprocity

Designing with the people most impacted by the work ensures shared ownership, ongoing consent, and a cadence that can last beyond the initial engagement.

Attunement

We read the room, respect the story so far, and calibrate tools based on the rhythms and capacity of each team.

Emergence

Structured experiments invite surprise, making space for new roles and relationships to take root as conditions evolve.

Engagement Framework

Each partnership unfolds across four modular phases. We adapt the timing and depth of each to reflect your current capacity and desired outcomes.

Field Scan

Conduct interviews, ritual audits, and ecosystem mapping to understand existing narratives and the relationships that sustain them.

Design Lab

Prototype new rituals, story beats, and governance agreements with key stakeholders in playful, low-risk environments.

Integration Sprint

Embed successful practices into your operations with facilitation, documentation, and accountability scaffolds.

Outcomes & Measures

We co-create metrics that honor both qualitative shifts and tangible benchmarks. These indicators serve as anchors for reflection and celebration across the life of the project.

Experience Index

Track participant sentiment and story resonance at every major milestone to ensure momentum stays aligned with purpose.

Practice Adoption

Measure the frequency and depth of new rituals to confirm they are embedded sustainably within your workflows.

Continuity Signals

Review archival documentation to confirm knowledge transfer and shared ownership persist beyond the initial cycle.
