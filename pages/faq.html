Origins & Philosophy

Where did Reincarne Logic begin?

We emerged from a cooperative of designers, facilitators, and story workers who sought to bridge ritual arts with organizational strategy. Our practice continues to evolve through partnerships that honor cultural context and collective wisdom.

What guides your approach?

We treat every engagement as a cycle. That means making space for emergence, reflection, and celebration while ensuring participants feel supported before, during, and after the work.

Services & Collaboration

Do you offer custom engagements?

Yes. We co-design each residency, workshop series, or strategic retreat to address your unique rhythms and goals.

Can you work alongside internal teams?

Absolutely. We often embed with staff to strengthen existing rituals and coach teams as they test new practices.

How do you measure success?

Together we define a blend of qualitative and quantitative indicators that reflect your desired shifts in culture, belonging, and operational flow.

Logistics

Where do you operate?

We collaborate with partners around the globe. Virtual residencies, hybrid gatherings, and in-person retreats are all available.

How far in advance should we reach out?

We typically book engagements two to three months ahead. Reach out as soon as you sense a new cycle emerging so we can co-design the right path forward.

What accessibility considerations do you honor?

Every offering includes collaborative access planning. We budget for interpretation, captioning, and care resources as part of our core delivery.
